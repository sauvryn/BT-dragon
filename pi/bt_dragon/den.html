Care

Sleep screen
Like an e-reader's: while he naps or sleeps, his screen shows this instead of whatever it showed last (e-ink keeps its picture even with the power off). Use your own pictures under Artwork.

Backup

A small file with everything that's yours: him, his diary, lands, the shopping list, calendar, voice profiles, your artwork, your settings and Developer Tools knobs. It includes your hotspot passwords and WiGLE token, so keep it somewhere private.

Without the den: copy a backup onto the card's boot partition (the one Windows can open), named harald-restore.tar.gz, and he restores it the next time he starts.

The moon

On the day of each new moon, first quarter, full moon and last quarter, Harald gets a burst of joy (the full moon is the biggest) and announces it: on his screen, and out loud if he has a voice. No internet needed: the phases are worked out on the Pi.

Ask Harald

Type a question and his answer appears below (and he says it out loud, if he has a voice). Try: the time in Tokyo, the date, "set a pasta timer for twelve minutes", what's on the shopping list or the calendar, the weather, or at home: "what's the temperature at home?", the humidity, air pressure, pollen count, UV index, air quality, sunrise or sunset.

He only knows a set range of questions (the full list is in the README), and his answers don't come from a search engine. For anything else, use a real search engine (DuckDuckGo, for example) or an assistant that uses one (Hey Google, Siri, Alexa and so on).

Set up the phone voice · once, in Termux
  1. From F-Droid, install Termux and Termux:API (both from F-Droid: the Play Store versions don't work together).
  2. Open Termux and type: pkg install -y python termux-api
  3. Then:
  4. In Android's settings, Apps → Termux → Battery: Unrestricted, so it keeps running with the screen off.

He says hello through the phone when it's working. Leave Termux running (its notification stays while it's listening). Next time, open Termux and type python harald_voice.py. The phone's media volume sets how loud he is, and Bluetooth earphones work too. Only the words he says go to the phone; nothing is kept.

Setup-mode rescue · after the Dot restarts

After a restart an unregistered Echo goes into setup mode (spinning orange ring), switches your hotspot off and broadcasts an open network of its own. With its name below, Harald hops onto it for a moment, checks it's your Dot, switches your hotspot back on and hops home. Look for the name in your phone's Wi-Fi list while the ring is orange (like "Amazon-XY7").

Find him by Wi-Fi (no GPS): off

For a dragon with no GPS. He sends the list of Wi-Fi networks he can hear to beaconDB, a free community service that knows roughly where routers are, and it answers with roughly where he is.

Less private: the networks around you are where you are. Every lookup tells beaconDB where he is, to within tens of meters, and its server logs keep your internet address and the time for 28 days. It only runs while there's no GPS and he's on your hotspot, at most every two minutes, and not while the same networks are around.

A Wi-Fi position claims lands and realms, spots landmarks and discovers green spaces. It never counts towards walk distances, walks, runs or rides, and nothing found this way is uploaded to WiGLE.
