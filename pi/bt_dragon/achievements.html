How achievements work

Achievements are milestones for how many of each thing has collected. They count things, not points: points are for titles.

Each one is earned in five stages, as in Bookworm: Copper, Bronze, Silver, Gold, then Platinum. Each stage is a runestone in its metal's colour, carved with the rune of its category: ᚷ (G, the Gebo rune) for Gadgets, ᛊ (S, the Sowilo rune) for Stars, and so on. No two categories share a rune, so where a first letter is already taken, the next fitting word gives it: N (Nature) for green spaces, P (Places) for famous places, A (Aircraft) for Sky Dragons.

The runes are the Elder Futhark, the oldest runic alphabet, carved in wood, metal and stone from about the 2nd to the 8th century. It's named after its first six letters, F U Th A R K, as "alphabet" is named after alpha and beta. Each rune was also a word that began with its sound.
