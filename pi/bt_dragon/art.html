Your own pictures in place of the drawn placeholder

Each picture is 96 × 86 pixels, black and white, saved as PNG (JPEG blurs edges into grey). One per mood; a second frame is optional, and the two alternate on screen updates (blinking, chomping, drifting Zzz). Moods without a picture keep the drawn version, so you can replace them one at a time. Anything the drawn one adds on top (Zzz, sparkles, sweat, speed lines) is part of your picture.

On the panel every pixel is ink or paper: transparent counts as paper, and grey becomes black if darker than half way, white if lighter. The previews below show exactly what the screen will show.

Template downloads the drawn placeholder at actual size, to draw over. Upload saves a picture into harald-art on the card's boot partition; you can also copy files there from Windows, named like happy.png, happy_1.png (second frame), happy-hungry.png.
